Privacy policy

Puckkoll · Last updated 31 October 2026 · Svenska

Puckkoll has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not know who you are. The app keeps most of what it knows on your phone. A small server that I run sends the notifications, and it stores only what it needs to do that. This page says exactly what, and why.

Who is responsible

Anders Johansson, a private person in Sweden, makes Puckkoll and runs its server. I am the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: see Questions below.

What stays on your phone

  • The clubs and players you follow, your notification choices, the language, the appearance, the table columns and your other settings.
  • A copy of the last schedule, so the app opens quickly.
  • Pictures you choose for clubs. They are picked with the system photo picker, so Puckkoll never gets access to your photo library and only receives the picture you choose. They are stored in the app's own storage and are never uploaded.
  • A random identifier for your phone, kept in the keychain. If you use iCloud Keychain, Apple syncs it between your own devices, which is how Tipset recognises you on a new phone. I cannot read it from Apple.
  • The Apple Watch app and the widgets get your followed clubs from the iPhone through Apple's own connection and an app group on the phone, so nothing extra leaves it.
  • Game summaries written with Apple Intelligence are produced on your iPhone. The text never leaves it.

What is sent to the Puckkoll server

The app registers with my server when it starts and when one of the values below changes. This is needed to send you notifications and Live Activities. Notifications are optional: until you allow them the server cannot send you anything, but the registration still includes your choices and an "off" mark.

DataWhy
The random phone identifierTo tell phones apart.
Apple's push tokens for notifications, Live Activities and starting a Live ActivityTo deliver notifications through Apple. A token is not a name or a number; Apple issues it to the app on your phone.
The clubs and players you followTo know which games to tell you about.
Your notification choices, the language and the delay settingTo send what you asked for, in your language.
The app version and build, and when the phone last connectedTo see that the app and server work together, and to remove phones that disappeared.

The server does not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or anything from other apps. It does not store your IP address. Its own log lists what happened (such as "a goal in a game") and the paths of requests with their result codes, not who made them. A short list of the latest notifications, with the first eight characters of the identifier, helps me find problems and covers the latest few hundred notifications.

Tipset

Tipset is for friends and family and is off until you opt in. If you do, the server gives you a random nickname and stores your tips, the groups you join or create (with their names and codes), the reactions you send and the awards you earn. The other members of your groups see your nickname, your points, and your tips when the game has started or as far as your own reveal setting allows. Nobody sees the identifier. Under Tipset you can change your nickname or leave Tipset, which removes your tips and group memberships from the server.

Where the data lives, and for how long

The server runs on a computer in my home in Sweden. It is reached through Cloudflare's tunnel service, which therefore handles your connection (and can see its technical data, such as the IP address) on the way. Cloudflare acts as my processor under its own terms. The server's data is copied once a day to a backup on the same computer, and the copies are kept for two weeks.

  • A phone's record is kept while you use the app. Phones that have not connected for a long time may be removed.
  • Tipset data is kept until you leave Tipset or ask me to delete it.

Other services the app talks to

  • shl.se, sdhl.se and hockeyallsvenskan.se. The app fetches schedules, results, tables and statistics directly from the leagues' own websites. They can see your IP address and the app's requests, as with any website, under their own privacy policies. Puckkoll is not affiliated with them.
  • Apple. Apple delivers notifications and Live Activities, distributes the app through TestFlight or the App Store, and may collect usage and crash data under Apple's own policy. I see only what Apple chooses to share, such as feedback you send through TestFlight.
  • Where to watch. A button for where to watch a game opens the broadcaster's website in your browser, which is then subject to its own policy.

I do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and there is no advertising or analytics in the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only if you allow them.
  • Calendar: write-only access, only when you choose to add a game. Puckkoll can add an event but never reads your calendar.
  • Photos: none. See above about the photo picker.
  • The app does not use your location, microphone, camera or contacts.

Your rights

You can ask me what the server has stored about you, ask me to correct or delete it, and withdraw consent for notifications at any time in the app or in iOS Settings. Because I cannot tell who a phone belongs to, I may ask you to show me the identifier from your own phone to find your record. The basis for the processing is your consent (notifications and Tipset) and my legitimate interest in running the app and keeping it secure. If you are unhappy with how I handle your data you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, IMY (imy.se).

Children

Puckkoll is not aimed at children, and it does not knowingly collect data about anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. If a change means collecting something new, the app will say so first.

Questions

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page and I will answer there. Please do not post your identifier or other personal details in a public issue: ask for another way to send them and I will arrange it.
