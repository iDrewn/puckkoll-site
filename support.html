Help with Puckkoll

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. If you are a TestFlight tester you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the app's version and build (under Mer) and what you were doing. Do not post personal details or your Tipset identity in a public issue.

Common questions

I do not get notifications.

Go to Mer → Notiser and switch on Notiser och Live Activities, and allow notifications when iOS asks. Then follow at least one club under Mina lag: notifications are sent for the clubs you follow. Check that the club is an SHL club and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not holding them back. Notifications and Live Activities come from the Puckkoll server; if the app shows a banner that the server is being serviced, they may be late for a while.

Why are there no notifications for SDHL?

The SDHL's website has the schedule, results, table and player statistics, but no goals or period data. Puckkoll can show all of that, and notifications, Live Activities and Tipset are for the SHL only for now.

How do I switch between SHL and SDHL?

Use the league menu in the top corner of Matcher and Tabell, or Mer → Utseende → Liga. The clubs you follow are remembered separately for each league.

What is Tipset?

A friendly tipping game. Tip the result of a game before puck drop: the right winner gives 2 points and the exact result 3 more. Create a group, share its code, and see the season and weekly boards. You are shown by a made-up nickname. It is off until you open the Tipset tab and join.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my Tipset?

If iCloud Keychain is on, your identity follows you to the new phone. If not, open Tipset on the old phone, choose Identitet och säkerhetskopia, copy the code and use it on the new phone under Jag har redan en identitet. Keep the code to yourself: whoever has it can act as you in Tipset.

How do I delete what the server knows about me?

In Tipset you can leave Tipset, which removes your tips and group memberships. To have the rest removed, ask in an issue and I will tell you how to send me your identity without making it public. Read the privacy policy.

Can I hide the results?

Yes. Turn on Spoilerfritt läge under Mer. Results of games that have started are hidden until you tap to reveal them. Notifications and Live Activities still show results: switch them off, or use the 30 second delay, for games you watch later.

How do I change the badges, or show more columns in the table?

Under Mer → Utseende you can pick Puck or Skiva med lagkod for the badges, and Egna lagbilder to put a picture of your own on a club. In Tabell, the table button in the toolbar opens the column picker. On a narrow screen only the most important columns fit; turn the phone on its side for more.

Why does a score look wrong for a few minutes?

Puckkoll reads the leagues' own websites, which are sometimes a little behind or briefly show a game as finished before the score is filled in. The app and server check the play-by-play and correct it as soon as the league does. If a result stays wrong, please report it with the game and the time.

Does Puckkoll use my photos or my location?

No. Pictures for clubs are chosen with the system photo picker, so the app never sees your library, and the app does not use your location.

Which languages does it support?

Swedish and English. Automatic follows your iPhone's language and can be changed under Mer → Utseende → Språk.
